## Bug Fixed (commit 6dd73b8)
**Admin tenant selection resetting on page navigation** (handoff #237 from QA)

Root cause: AdminModeProvider was duplicated in both AppLayout and AdminLayout.
Navigating between /admin/* and /app/* destroyed one provider and created another,
causing mode/tenant state to reset and triggering redirect effects that sent
admins to the onboarding/go-live page.

Fix:
1. Lifted AdminModeProvider to App.tsx as singleton (never remounts)
2. Removed from AppLayout and AdminLayout
3. Added localStorage backup check in redirect effects
